# Sources des kanjis / Kanji data sources

The 323 supplementary kanji entries use KANJIDIC2, copyright Jim Breen and
the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG).

- Dictionary: https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/KANJIDIC_Project.html
- Download: https://www.edrdg.org/kanjidic/kanjidic2.xml.gz
- Source release: 2026-09-08, database version 2026-251.
- Licensing notice: https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
- Supplementary data and its translations/adaptations: CC BY-SA 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Japanese on/kun readings are copied from the corresponding Japanese reading
fields, without Chinese readings or name-only readings. English meanings come
from KANJIDIC2; four documented editorial adaptations clarify 身, 璧, 芯 and 鹸.
French glosses are editorial translations/adaptations of these sourced senses.
KANJIDIC2 supplies French glosses for 35 of these entries, not all 323.
These translations have not been independently certified by a lexicographer.
JLPT placement is our pedagogical classification based on the vocabulary's
KanjiLevel, not a claim that these are official JLPT kanji levels.

Les 323 fiches supplémentaires utilisent les lectures japonaises et les sens
anglais de KANJIDIC2. Les sens français sont des traductions/adaptations
éditoriales ; 35 fiches disposent aussi de sens français dans le dictionnaire
source. Les quatre précisions de sens et leurs références sont conservées dans
le manifeste du projet, ainsi que les empreintes des sources. Le classement
est pédagogique et fondé sur KanjiLevel, pas un classement JLPT officiel.
